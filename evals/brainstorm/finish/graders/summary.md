The workspace holds a finished brainstorm for a gym app. Purpose: a business selling a subscription to gyms. Who it serves: members of independent gyms (one or two locations), with gym owners paying. Goal: keep members coming back. Features: weekly progress emails and a streak dashboard. Roadmap: an MVP in 3 months with progress emails for one pilot gym, then a version 2 with the streak dashboard. Risk: gym owners may not adopt it. Assumption: owners will switch it on if setup takes under ten minutes.
